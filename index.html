THE KNOWLEDGE COLLECTION

Everyday protection.
Exceptional confidence.

Explore six essential topics designed to make digital security understandable and actionable.

01 / ESSENTIALS ✦

Spot Phishing

Learn to recognize suspicious messages, fake websites, and urgent requests designed to steal your information.

Practical safety tip

Open the official app or type the website address yourself instead of following unexpected links.

02 / ESSENTIALS ◈

Stronger Passwords

Discover why long, unique passwords and password managers are essential for protecting your accounts.

Practical safety tip

Use a different password for every important account and enable multi-factor authentication.

03 / ESSENTIALS ◎

Account Protection

Understand multi-factor authentication, verification codes, and how to respond to unexpected sign-in alerts.

Practical safety tip

Never share a one-time verification code with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

04 / EVERYDAY SAFETY ◇

Digital Privacy

Take control of the personal information you share, app permissions, and your online footprint.

Practical safety tip

Review app permissions regularly and avoid posting details that could help someone impersonate you.

05 / EVERYDAY SAFETY ⌁

Safer Online Banking

Identify financial scams, monitor transactions, and verify requests through trusted banking channels.

Practical safety tip

Turn on transaction alerts and contact your bank using its official number if something looks wrong.

06 / EVERYDAY SAFETY ⬡

Device Security

Keep your phone and computer protected with updates, screen locks, and safer connection habits.

Practical safety tip

Install updates promptly and avoid sensitive activity on public or shared devices.
